The engagement roadmap

From kickoff to hand-off, and beyond

Scroll to follow a project step by step, from the first call to the systems, brand, or site running on their own.

01

Discovery Call

We start with a conversation, your goals, pain points, and what "better" looks like for your business.

  • Current workflows, tools, and pain points
  • Goals for the engagement and what success looks like
  • Fit, scope, and next steps
~30–45 min call
02

Proposal & Roadmap

You get a clear scope, timeline, and investment laid out plainly, no surprises before anything begins.

  • Defined scope and deliverables
  • Project timeline and milestones
  • Transparent investment, no hidden fees
Delivered in a few days
03

Kickoff

We align on access, communication rhythm, and what success looks like for the engagement.

  • Access to accounts, tools, and stakeholders
  • Communication rhythm and check-in cadence
  • Roles and responsibilities on both sides
Week one
04

Build & Implement

We design and build the systems, workflows, brand, or site laid out in the roadmap.

  • Systems, workflows, brand, or site development
  • Regular progress updates
  • Milestone check-ins along the way
The bulk of the engagement
05

Review & Refine

You test it, we gather feedback, and refine the details before anything goes live.

  • Walkthrough and testing together
  • Feedback rounds and adjustments
  • Final quality check before launch
1–2 feedback rounds
06

Launch

Everything goes live, documented and ready for your team to run with.

  • Go-live and final checks
  • Documentation handed over
  • Walkthrough with your team
Launch day

Where the engagement goes from here

07A

Hand-off

A clean transfer of ownership, documentation, and training, you're fully equipped to run it yourselves.

  • Full ownership transfer
  • Documentation and training included
  • Available for questions after launch
One-time engagement
07B

Ongoing Support

Recurring support built around the systems we put in place, so you're never without a partner.

  • Recurring check-ins and maintenance
  • Priority support for issues or changes
  • Continued optimization over time
Recurring partnership
